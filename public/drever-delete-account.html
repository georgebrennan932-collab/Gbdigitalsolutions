You can request permanent deletion of your Drever account and the personal data associated with your account.

How to request account deletion

  1. Contact GB Digital Solutions using the contact form on our website.
  2. Use the email address associated with your Drever account.
  3. Include the words "Delete my Drever account" in your message.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you are the owner of the account before completing the deletion request.

What will be deleted?

Following verification of your request, your Drever account and associated personal information will be permanently deleted, including information connected with your Drever profile and use of the service.

Certain information may be retained where GB Digital Solutions is required to keep it for legal, regulatory, security or financial purposes.
